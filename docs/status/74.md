
Entry 74 (§75) is implemented as follows.

- **Part A** is in Sugar (`liftLoop`, `obsFrame`, the lend per observation `TurnLend`/`MTurn`)
  and Effects (`loopsAt`, the clause classes).
- **Part B**:
  - The pieces are in `lib/pipe.bc`: `pipe_step`, `pipe_stop`, `pipe_with_stop`, `pipe_forward`,
    `pipe_finish`, `pipe_handoff`, the storing and cancelling clauses, and `connect`, `close` and
    `pipe_source`. They are §76 templates, with plain κ.
  - The Effects pass does the following:
    - `connectorV` puts each stage at sites of its own and handles forwarding;
    - `forChain` makes a chain the generator of a `for`;
    - `streamChain` handles `connect`;
    - a chain's inferred row leaves out its wires;
    - `Fins` reads the connector node for the cancel rows of the forwarded and passed-through
      operations.
  - The generator is in two places:
    - Sugar's `connParts`/`connView` read each stage's loop, rows and cancel rows off the stage as
      elaborated;
    - `Frontend/Elab/Connector.lean` writes the glue: the state tuple, the three frame lists, one
      `unfold`, and a pull-mode glue for `connect`'s rest.
  - `RowInst.runMore` makes the glue's uses of the pieces into instances.
  - `forYield` takes the connector's maker as a generator.
- **Programs:**
  - core text: `corpus/core-pipe-pieces.bc` and `corpus/pipe-pieces-step.bc`;
  - the seven law traces, in `corpus/pipe-close-order.bc`, `pipe-raise-order.bc`,
    `pipe-cancel-order.bc` and `pipe-first-error.bc`;
  - also `corpus/pipe-connector.bc`, `pipe-pushback.bc`, `pipe-rest.bc` and `pipe-stream-edge.bc`;
  - the rejects `corpus/reject/pipe-fwd-two.bc` and `pipe-lend-observation.bc`;
  - the book's *Stream stages* (`book/src/guide/iteration.md`).
- **Open:**

  - their counts-backend2 rows are pending.
