- **1.93** (2026-11-10) — §41 (#1053): *Open* lists two known wrong answers of `let mut`. The
  first is a write lost in a finaliser form's continuation inside a loop, an `if` arm or a
  binding's head. The second is a loop's writes lost under a cancel clause that drops `k`, where
  the loop's body can abort. A redesign of `let mut` over a `State` effect is under study.
