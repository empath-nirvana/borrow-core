- **1.86** (2026-11-10) — §15, §12, §27 (#910): stored generators over a row. A generator is stored
  whatever else it performs, at `pull_Σ A B R`; an observation and `close` perform `Σ`. A stored
  generator over a row, or a value built from such generators, is dropped at its row by a close:
  at scope end, as in Rust, after a path's last effect; and at a tail call just before the call,
  as Rust's `become` does (RFC 3407), when every value the call takes is bound by its run-free
  argument bindings or can be dropped by a raise. The adapters are copied at their source's row;
  a written stored-generator type gets its row by inference. No Core change.
