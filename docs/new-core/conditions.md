# The new Core: conditions and gaps

Every side condition the design of SPEC §62 carries, and every gap it has, classified. Lean names
are in `evidence.md`.

Classes: SEMANTIC (programs need it; justified) · PROOF (needed to make a proof close; to be
derived or justified) · DESIGN (a choice the design makes) · GAP (not built) · RISK (could fail
later).

| # | Item | Class | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | No unrestricted (multi-shot-capable) operation while a borrow or owned resource is live | SEMANTIC | Justified (argued): a lend live across a stop puts the lender's exclusive cell in the continuation's share; an unrestricted continuation resumed twice would lend twice, consumed it would leak. That its share owns nothing is a clause of the share's definition (`contShareN`), not a result. Multi-shot continuations over duplicable state are a hypothesis, not a rule. |
